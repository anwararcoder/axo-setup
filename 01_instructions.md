How Axo works

What is Axo?
Axo is a small private AI challenge group where participants work on practical AI/ML problems.

Who can join: Axo accepts members who subscribe to Claude Pro or Max, specifically so they can use Opus 5.5. Members on the free Claude plan are not accepted.

Each challenge may include:
• a dataset,
• a challenge description,
• evaluation requirements,
• and an AI Baseline.

The goal is to build a solution that performs better than the challenge's AI Baseline while still following the challenge rules.

AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor and Copilot are allowed in every challenge. You do not need to understand every detail 100%, but you should know how to use AI well to get the best result, and follow the challenge rules.

The objective is an effective, clean, reasonable solution, not unnecessary complexity.

AI Baseline
The AI Baseline is the benchmark/reference score used to decide whether you have beaten the challenge's AI benchmark.

• Beating the public score does not guarantee a strong private result.
• The real objective is good generalization. Build the solution to work on unseen data, not only the visible development data.

Public and private data
A challenge may contain a public portion and a private portion.

• Public portion: used for development, experimentation, testing and iteration.
• Private portion: hidden from participants and used for the private/final evaluation. The separation reduces overfitting and tests whether the solution generalizes.

Private data must never be shared, published, redistributed or exposed.

Choosing a challenge
You decide which challenge you want to work on. Challenge availability can change. A challenge may become full, change state, close, or otherwise become unavailable. Do not assume a challenge you picked will stay open until you submit.

Solver capacity is currently 10. A challenge that fills up does not simply close: it goes through the grace hour, lockdown and top 10 round explained next. Do not assume a challenge can be started later.

When a challenge fills up: grace hour, lockdown, top 10
When all 10 solver slots are taken, a challenge goes through three steps:

1. Grace hour (exactly 1 hour, counted from the moment the slots fill). Anyone can still submit and edit. You do not need an earlier submission, and it does not matter whether you are above or below the AI Baseline. The Start New Solution button stays available. The cutoff is the end of the hour: a submission sent before it gets up to one more hour to finish scoring, but scoring takes about 15-30 minutes, so send your work early.
2. Lockdown hour (up to 1 hour). Nobody can submit or edit at all. The platform picks the top 10 from the scores that are ready, and the Start New Solution button is hidden until it is over.
3. Continuing round. Only those top 10 keep submitting, until the challenge closes. If Axo is among them the button comes back and you can submit new versions as usual. If Axo is not, the challenge is over for us.

The channel post and the status in the comments always show which step a challenge is in, and /submit only offers challenges that can take a submission right now.





After a successful scored submission
When your upload is accepted and its score is successfully calculated:
• the submission can appear on the leaderboard,
• you can keep improving according to the challenge state,
• and if the challenge fills up, the grace hour applies to everyone, and if Axo then makes the top 10 you can keep improving in the continuing round.

Always follow the actual state shown in the channel and by the bot. A full solver count does not mean the work is over: check which step the challenge is in.

Closing time
Every challenge also has a Closing Time. This is the point when the challenge closes completely. After it closes, the private leaderboard/result is announced. Submit and improve before the applicable deadline.

Private leaderboard requirement
You cannot access the private/final result unless you made a submission and the solution's score was successfully calculated. An upload attempt is not a successful submission.
