Axo rules

Confidentiality
This is one of the strongest Axo rules. Challenge datasets are private. So are challenge descriptions and materials, private challenge links, submission files, submitted solution/code, private evaluation data and all other Axo challenge materials.

You must not:
• share challenge data with another person,
• publish Axo datasets or upload them to GitHub or Kaggle,
• post Axo data in Discord or Telegram groups,
• publish it on public drives or social media,
• distribute Axo challenge files to another community,
• or redistribute private Axo materials in any form.

Consequence: anyone who shares or redistributes private Axo materials may be permanently blacklisted from future Axo challenges and may lose participation or reward eligibility.

AI tool usage
Paid and free AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot and others) are allowed in every challenge and can be part of your workflow. You may not fully understand every part of a challenge, and that is fine, but you should know how to use AI well to get the best possible result. You must:
• use AI tools skillfully and check what they produce,
• follow the challenge rules,
• review generated code,
• and avoid blindly submitting code you have not checked.

Do not submit copied work from another participant or an existing competition solution when the challenge rules prohibit it. The goal is to solve the challenge effectively, not to generate a large amount of code.

Claude Pro or Max is required
Axo accepts members who subscribe to Claude Pro or Max, specifically so they can use Opus 5.5. Members on the free Claude plan are not accepted. Members found to be on the free plan may be removed.

Participant responsibility
You are responsible for:
• choosing your challenge,
• reading and understanding the challenge rules,
• respecting confidentiality,
• following the submission format,
• picking the right challenge when you submit,
• understanding your own solution,
• keeping copies of your own code and submission until the process is complete,
• and submitting within the available challenge window.
You also decide whether to keep improving one challenge or move to another.

No guarantee
Joining Axo does not guarantee acceptance of every submission, participation after applying, a reward, a strong private score, continued challenge availability, or a specific ranking. A challenge can change state or become unavailable, and beating a public score does not guarantee a strong private result. Nothing is promised before the actual state or result supports it.
