Additional $70 pool
There is a separate additional pool of exactly $70, for eligible participants ranked 4th or lower. To qualify you must:
• be ranked 4th or lower, and
• have a score strictly greater than the AI Baseline.

The $70 is distributed dynamically from the actual eligible leaderboard results. Higher-ranked eligible participants receive larger or equal rewards than lower-ranked eligible participants, and the total distributed is exactly $70 when eligible participants exist. If nobody is eligible, the additional pool is not distributed.

Reward disclaimer
No reward amount is promised before the actual leaderboard/result is available. This is not a winner-takes-all system: participants who meet the applicable conditions can be rewarded even outside the top 3. If a challenge has different or additional challenge-specific rewards, the challenge's own rules apply.

See /my_payout for your own earnings and payout timing.
