Welcome to Axo 👋

Axo is a small private group for practical AI/ML challenges. You work on real problems, build a solution, and submit your prediction file and solution code.

Who can join: Axo accepts members who subscribe to Claude Pro or Max, specifically so they can use Opus 5.5. Members on the free Claude plan are not accepted.

To get into the group:
1. Tap Join the Axo group below. The bot then shows you the information pages one by one.
2. Read every page. The Next button only unlocks after enough time to actually read the page.
3. After the last page, tap the start button to begin a short quiz (8 questions).
4. Answer all 8 correctly. Options are shuffled every attempt, and after a wrong answer you wait 20 seconds before trying again.

Read everything carefully: you will be tested on it, so do not just click through. You can also read /instructions and /rules any time.

Available commands
