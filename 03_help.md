Axo Commands

/start - Start Axo or see the welcome message
/instructions - How Axo challenges work
/submit - How to prepare and submit your work
/prizes - Current prize and reward structure
/my_work - Check your challenge and submission status
/my_payout - Check payout information
/rules - Important rules and responsibilities
/news - Updates and announcements, by version
/cancel - Cancel the submission you are in the middle of

Use the command that matches what you need.

Full challenges: when a challenge fills up it goes through a 1-hour grace hour (anyone can still submit), a lockdown of up to 1 hour (nobody can) and then a round for the top 10 only. The steps are explained in /instructions, and the channel post shows which one a challenge is in.
